This repo contains mechanical drawings, assemblies, and part files for miscellaneous projects around Hat Creek Radio Observatory. Parts created with AutoDesk Inventor 2024/25 and Front Panel Designer.

## Index

### GOES Antenna Mount
Custom mount for [Nooelec GOES grid antenna](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B08HGQXC7C/ref=as_li_ss_tl?smid=A2JO7YP9I9Y3D6&psc=1&linkCode=sl1&tag=rsv0f-20&linkId=0e1541f0a7610de1997e308c836bce88&language=en_US) used in ARISE Lab [Decoding Earth](https://agiseti.com/index.html).

### HCRO ALPACA Shed
Documents relating to construction of the shed that houses [ALPACA](https://gitlab.ras.byu.edu/alpaca/homepage).

### PCB Holder
Small plate to hold [PAX PCBs](https://github.com/SETIatHCRO/Front-Page/tree/master/Analog-Signal-Components/PAX/PAM-2025) within stencil frame fixture for solder paste application.
