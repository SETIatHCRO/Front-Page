TEC driver card, linear version

Input/output list

Connector 1 - 15 pin sub D
female on board, male on cable going to power supply and PAX Case cable
all cable wires 24 ga except as noted (20 ga is 0.01 ohm/ft)

pin	function
1	NC or shield
2	PAX thermistor +
9	PAX thermistor -
3	airstream (aux1) thermistor +
10	airstream (aux1) thermistor -
4	TEC heatsink (aux2) thermistor +
11	TEC heatsink (aux2) thermistor -	  
6	supply +24 V		(20 ga wire in cable)
13	supply ground		(20 ga wire in cable)
8	TEC drive -		(20 ga wire in cable)
15	TEC drive +		(20 ga wire in cable)



Connector 2 - 9 pin sub D
female on board, male on cable to Rim Box controller board 

pin	function
1	setpoint voltage
2	rimbox heatsink thermistor monitor
6	ground
3	PAX thermistor monitor
7	ground
4	airstream (aux1) thermistor monitor
8	ground
5	TEC heatsink (aux2) thermistor monitor
9	ground
