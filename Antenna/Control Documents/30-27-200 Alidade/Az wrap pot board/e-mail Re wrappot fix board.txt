Thank you for the thorough work and the good news with regard to the prototype wrappot fix board.  I added two columns to your spreadsheet (see attached image):  column C computes azimuth from the wrappot value in column B (given current intercept and slope) and column D computes the absolute value difference between encoder azimuth (column A) and wrappot azimuth (column C).

The largest azimuth difference is 7 degrees.  RMS is probably 2 to 3 degrees (I did not compute it).  This is as good as can be expected given current 5/3 azimuth-to-wrappot gear ratio, wrappot fix board gain, and ADC resolution.  AND, much more accurate than what is needed for wrap determination and 3f,3g,3h collision avoidance.
