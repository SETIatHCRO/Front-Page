Hi Dave,
Board sensor (my boards) are Microchip TC77 surface mount.  All other external sensors are LM35 in TO-220 package.  TO-220 package is the same package commonly used for linear regulators: large tab with central 150 mil hole that should be easy to mount with a screw or bolt in most places.  LM35 were selected for wide temp. range. Celsius calibration, multi-sourced industry standard, easy mounting, and most importantly:  
analog return considering they will be external in some cases to the EE and drive boxes.
