Hi All

I added a command to query the alarm status of the array. This command returns messages if, at the time of last measurement, any of the following parameters are out of range: ControlBoxTemp, DriveBoxTemp, RimBoxTemp, PAXBoxTemp, CryoTemp, AzDriveCurrent, ElDriveCurrent. It also checks the status of the servo motors and overall antenna status. 

The command is "ataalarms -l".

These data are returned in a listing grouped by antenna. For example at 5:30 Friday night we obtain:

  ant1f:
          CryoTemp (146.58827429848444) is out of range {60.0,63.0)
  ant2d:
          CryoTemp (291.15985165721315) is out of range {60.0,63.0)
  ant2g:
          DriveBoxTemp (63.8) is out of range {-20.0,40.0)
  ant2j:
          RimBoxTemp (40.2) is out of range {-20.0,40.0)
  ant2m:
          CryoTemp (298.3992708012115) is out of range {60.0,63.0)
  ant3j:
          No reading for rim box temperature.
          No reading for pax box temperature.
          No reading for cryo dewar temperature.
  ant4h:
          CryoTemp (288.6901539804845) is out of range {60.0,63.0)
  ant5c:
          CryoTemp (294.17627630054585) is out of range {60.0,63.0)
  ant5e:
          CryoTemp (348.13257210815897) is out of range {60.0,63.0)
  ant5g:
          CryoTemp (289.9532817998801) is out of range {60.0,63.0)



It lists 7 CryoTemp's, one DriveBoxTemp, one RimBoxTemp as out of range. We already knew that 3j is awaiting maintenance.

Possibly this command can be incorporated into the daily test observations.
