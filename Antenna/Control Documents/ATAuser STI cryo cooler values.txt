  Hi Matt

  Here is a snapshot of the STI cryo powers and dewar temperatures for our antennas. Most of the cryos are working fine with stable temperatures of 62 K and powers in the range of 30-40 W.

  Antenna 4k might have the wrong set point: it is stable at 65 K. Antennas with higher temperatures are probably "out of regulation", and generally have higher power values as they try to cool back down.

  There are two new commands to retrieve these values. For example, "atagetcryopower 4k" and "atagetcryotemp 4k". These commands take a string of antennas, too (e.g. 1a,1b,1c,1d...). By default, every five minutes both of these values are logged in the AntennaServer, eventually they will be stored in the ATA database. To get a recent history (last 24 hr) of values use

    atagetitem 4k cryotemp -h
    atagetitem 4k cryopower -h
